Money has two faces

For 2,600 years the front of money, its obverse, has carried the face of whoever made it. Emperors, kings, gods. The reverse said what it was worth.

We turned it around. The front of an Obverse card carries one face: yours.

The front is yours

No number, no expiry date, no bank logo the size of a billboard. Brushed steel, a chip, and your name, cut into the metal by laser.

  • Engraved, not printed, so your name never wears off
  • Pays by tap or chip anywhere cards are taken

Questions

Does contactless work through steel?

Yes. The antenna sits in the carbon core and the steel is cut around it, so the card pays by tap at any contactless terminal, the same as plastic.

Can I change the finish later?

Once a year at no cost. Send the old card back in the sleeve we include and we recycle its steel.

What happens if I lose it?

Freeze it in the app straight away. A replacement reaches you within two days, and your virtual numbers keep working in the meantime.

Why is the number on the back?

Because the front is the side people see. Keeping the number, expiry and code on the reverse means a glance across a table shows your name and nothing else.

Is Obverse a real card?

Not yet. This page is a design concept. Nothing on it is an offer of credit, and no card will be issued from it.
